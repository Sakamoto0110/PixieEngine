activate local body gravity in the runtime. The 2026-11-02 report remains the baseline for the

## Boundaries

- The collinear two-gravity case has a native reference because a different `gravityScale`
  reproduces its second body's acceleration. The non-collinear `(0,10)` / `(4,0)` observation
  still has no equivalent one-World global-gravity reference; it recorded six cross-Scene
  touching steps, without a comparative PASS claim.
- The deferred case records a request in a Box2D pre-solve callback and applies the fixture's
  membership/gravity change after the complete native step. It does not call or qualify the
  runtime's `World::SetScope` deferred API.
- The probe does not test motors, particle pools, project scenes, editor flows, or the full
  continuous collision trajectory. Its CCD check detects a full crossing of the thin floor.
  The technique remains experimental; dynamic bodies continue on the legacy runtime path.
