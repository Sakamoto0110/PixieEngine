In the editor: point lists in the inspector (rows of X and Y, add and remove, one step per change) and paths outlined when selected. Out: routing wires, the screen layer, text (PIXIE-TODO-030).

## Choices made while building it

- A path's points live in the entity's space and follow its transform; its width does not scale,
  so a wire keeps its thickness when its entity is scaled. A new `Path` has two points, 64 units
  apart, so adding the component shows something to grab.
- Joins are round because each segment is drawn as a capsule; two capsules overlapping at a join
  show it a little stronger when the colors are translucent. Opaque wires, the expected use, show
  nothing.
- The fill is measured along the rounded line, so a front moves at the same speed through corners.
  A fill of 1 also paints the round cap at the end.
- Paths are drawn between the sprites of negative layers and the rest: the sprite renderer flushes
  the layers below 0, the paths are drawn, then the other sprites. Picking walks the same order
  backwards: shapes, sprites of layer 0 or more, paths (the newest first), background sprites.
- A list of points in the inspector is drawn by the panel, as text is: a row per point, committed
  when the row lets go of the keyboard; adding and removing are steps of their own, through the same
  `Authoring` methods the scripted commands use.
