# Isolated local gravity probe — 2026-11-02

This is developer qualification evidence for an experimental Box2D force
technique. It is not approval to connect that technique to the runtime.

## Provenance and commands

- Source: `tests/qualification/local-gravity/main.cpp` and its standalone
  `CMakeLists.txt`, using the vendored `external/box2d` target without solver
  edits. CMake used Visual Studio 18 2026, x64; MSVC 19.51.36260.0.
- Configure: `cmake -S tests/qualification/local-gravity -B .local/validation/local-gravity-probe/build -G "Visual Studio 18 2026" -A x64`
- Build: `cmake --build .local/validation/local-gravity-probe/build --target local-gravity-probe --config Debug -j 4`, then the same command with `Release`.
- Run: `.local/validation/local-gravity-probe/build/{Debug,Release}/local-gravity-probe.exe`.
- Both builds and both runs exited 0. Complete per-case CSV is in
  `RESULTS-Debug-2026-11-02.csv` and `RESULTS-Release-2026-11-02.csv`. The two configurations
  reported identical numbers.

The contact cases reported equal touching pair sets, equal Box2D contact
counts, and equal cumulative begin/end event counts on every step. Event
mismatch steps were zero for every case. Peak touching pairs were 1 for the floor/CCD/transfer/
sleep cases and 2 for the cross-scope case. Neither World crossed the far side
of the CCD floor. The sleep fixture observed a sleeping body and zero
unexpected wake steps. The transfer fixture preserved body and shape IDs,
pose, velocity and its contact set at the edit boundary. A separate
observational fixture with different gravity vectors `(0,10)` and `(4,0)`
recorded six steps with a cross-scope touching pair.

## Remaining gaps

- The different-gravity contact case has no equivalent one-World
  global-gravity reference, so its contact behavior is observational only.
- The transfer fixture calls `SetAwake(true)` once. Transfer of a sleeping
  body is not exercised.
- These fixtures use Box2D directly. They do not exercise deferred runtime
  membership, `NeedsSimulation()`, a full sweep of CCD trajectories, motors,
  particles, editor workflows or persisted scenes.
- No change to the normal physics path was made. The measured outcomes do not
  authorize force compensation, solver edits or activation of local body
  gravity.
